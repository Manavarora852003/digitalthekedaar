How Digital Thekedaar works

01 · Worker registers

Worker adds category, skills, experience, location and rates. Admin verifies the profile.

02 · Customer searches

Customer compares worker profiles, ratings, experience and rates.

03 · Hire & track

Customer sends a job request. Worker accepts/rejects and both sides can track the job.
